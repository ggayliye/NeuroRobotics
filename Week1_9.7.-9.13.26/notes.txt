->Watched a video about it.
-> universal open standard for wireless inductive charging created by the Wireless Power Consortium.
	- Electromagnetic Induction: A transmitter coil in the charging pad creates a magnetic field, and a receiver coil in your device converts that field back into an electric current to charge the battery
	- Digital Handshake: The charger sends low-power pings to detect a device, validates the object, and negotiates power levels before transferring full energy
	- Safety and Control: The system continuously monitors voltage, current, and battery temperature to prevent overheating and overcharging

-> Performance Disadvantages
	- Slower Speeds: Wired charging (like USB-C Power Delivery) easily reaches 30W to 100W+, while standard Qi charging is usually capped at 5W to 15W.
	- High Heat Generation: Energy lost during wireless transmission converts into heat, which can degrade your device's battery health over time if it gets too hot.
	- Poor Energy Efficiency: Wireless charging wastes roughly 20% to 30% more power than a cable because energy is lost as heat and electromagnetic dissipation.
-> Usability Disadvantages
	- Precise Alignment Required: If your phone is placed even slightly off-center on a standard Qi pad, it will charge incredibly slowly or stop charging entirely.
	- Restricted Phone Use: You cannot easily pick up, hold, or use your phone to text or play games while it is flat on a charging pad.
	- Thick Case Interference: Heavy-duty, rugged, or metallic phone cases block the magnetic fields and prevent the charger from working.

TASK 5: 2-metal pads charging /Magnetic Pogo Pin Charger/connector
Magnetic pogo pin chargers use spring-loaded pins and magnets to provide fast, snap-on connections that protect device ports from wear.

-> Key Advantages
• Easy Alignment: Magnets snap into place automatically without manual adjustment or looking.
• Port Protection: Reduces physical wear from plugging and unplugging traditional cables.
• Safe Breakaway: The magnetic connection detaches easily if the cord gets pulled or tripped over, preventing the device from falling.
• Water and Dust Resistance: Flat contact surfaces and sealed designs make it easier to achieve high waterproof ratings like IP67 or IP68.
• High Durability: Spring mechanisms and gold plating handle thousands of connection cycles and resist corrosion

-> "uses direct physical contact between two metal contact points on a device and two matching metal pins or pads on a charging dock to transfer electricity"
-> this method requires physical touch between the charger's metal terminals and the device's conductive pads
-> When the two metal surfaces align, they close an electrical circuit allowing direct current (DC) to flow straight into the device's battery
-> common usage: 
• smartwatches, rechargeable flashlights, electric toothbrushes, and certain laptop docking stations or game controller docks.
